Pricing
Run-length bands

Short runs pay the full rate plus the set-up fee. Longer runs get the reduction for their band on every metre. Past the last band, milling is charged as a share of what the raw stock costs us.

The quote above previews your changes before you save.

The milling charge covers the mill's running costs for the time a profile takes on the moulder in that hardwood (electric, the mill team and the yard team who feed it) and wear on the cutters, plus a margin. Short runs add a set-up fee, longer runs get a reduction, and bespoke profiles add the cost of new cutters. The hardwood itself is charged separately.

Raw stock is the customer's finished thickness × the finished width plus the hardwood's width allowance × the metres. Allow extra if the boards need cross-cutting for defects.
