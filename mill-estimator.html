Hardwoods (prices & waste)

Cost price is what you pay per m³ of raw hardwood. Width waste is added to the customer's finished width to get the raw width you cut from, so 150 mm at 30% means 195 mm of raw board. Thickness allowance (mm) and length waste (%) are there if you want them; leave them at 0 to allow for width only. Feed speed, cutter hours and set-up hours work the same way as in the softwood table.

Extras & pricing
